About Tanya

My Journey

From Passion to Profession

01

Born and brought up in Uttar Pradesh, my passion for painting began in childhood. I pursued Engineering after school, but eventually realised that art was where my true talent and passion lay.

02

After college, I completed a Diploma in Fine Arts and began seriously pursuing my artistic journey.

03

Drawn towards spirituality, I initially found myself painting Indian gods and goddesses.

04

I began sharing my artwork on Instagram and Facebook, where it gradually reached a wider audience.

05

As commissions grew, my passion for painting naturally transformed into a career in art. My painting journey took a professional turn in 2017, and I began teaching in 2022.
