About Tanya

My Journey

From Passion to Profession

Born and brought up in Uttar Pradesh, my passion for painting began in childhood. I pursued Engineering after school, but eventually realised that art was where my true talent and passion lay.

After college, I completed a Diploma in Fine Arts and began seriously pursuing my artistic journey.

Drawn towards spirituality, I initially found myself painting Indian gods and goddesses. I began sharing my artwork on Instagram and Facebook, where it gradually reached a wider audience.

As commissions grew, my passion for painting naturally transformed into a career in art.
